# Brisa K2 air purifier: frequently asked questions

Fictional product and company (Nimbel Home), written for testing.

## How large a room does the Brisa K2 clean?

The Brisa K2 is made for rooms up to 42 square metres. In a room of that size it changes the air about four times an hour on the highest fan speed.

## How often do I change the filters?

The Brisa K2 has two filters. The pre-filter is washable: rinse it every 2 weeks and let it dry fully. The combined HEPA and carbon filter cannot be washed and should be replaced every 9 months, or sooner when the filter light turns amber. A replacement filter, part number K2-F9, costs 39 dollars.

## How loud is it?

On sleep mode the Brisa K2 makes 24 decibels, about as loud as a whisper. On the highest speed it makes 52 decibels.

## How much electricity does it use?

It uses 6 watts on sleep mode and 48 watts on the highest speed.

## What does the coloured ring mean?

The ring shows air quality: blue is good, violet is moderate and red is poor. The ring can be dimmed or switched off by holding the light button for 3 seconds.

## Does it connect to an app?

Yes. The Nimbel Home app connects over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only; 5 GHz networks are not supported. The app shows the filter life and lets you set a timer of 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours.

## What is the warranty?

The warranty is 3 years for the motor and 1 year for all other parts. Filters are not covered. To claim, write to care@nimbel-home.example with the serial number, which is printed under the base.

## Can I return it?

You can return the purifier within 45 days of delivery if it is in its original box. Return shipping costs 15 dollars unless the unit is faulty.

## Troubleshooting

If the purifier will not start, check that the back cover is pressed fully closed: a safety switch stops the fan when the cover is open. If the filter light stays amber after a new filter is fitted, hold the filter button for 5 seconds to reset it.
