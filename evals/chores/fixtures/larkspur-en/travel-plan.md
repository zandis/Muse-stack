# Trip plan: Saltmarsh Coast, 18 to 23 May 2031

A fictional itinerary written for testing. The towns, hotels and operators are invented.

Travellers: Mira Okonjo-Vale and Bram Oyelaran.

## Day 1, Sunday 18 May: Port Aldermoor to Wrenhaven

Take the 08:40 Coastline Express from Port Aldermoor Central, platform 6. It arrives in Wrenhaven at 11:55. The tickets cost 34 dollars each and the booking reference is CX-77204. Check in at the Tidewatch Inn after 3 pm; two nights are booked at 112 dollars a night, breakfast included.

## Day 2, Monday 19 May: Wrenhaven

Morning: guided walk on the salt flats with Reedline Tours, starting at 9:30 am from the harbour office. The walk costs 28 dollars per person and lasts two and a half hours. Bring waterproof boots. Afternoon: the lighthouse museum, which closes at 5 pm and is closed on Tuesdays.

## Day 3, Tuesday 20 May: Wrenhaven to Osprey Island

Check out by 11 am. The ferry to Osprey Island leaves at 1:15 pm from pier 2 and takes 50 minutes. A return ferry ticket costs 19 dollars. Stay three nights at Gullrock Cabins, cabin number 8, at 95 dollars a night. The cabins have no restaurant; the island shop closes at 6 pm.

## Day 4, Wednesday 21 May: Osprey Island

Rent bicycles from the island shop for 12 dollars a day each. Ride to the north cliffs to see the seabird colony; the path is 14 kilometres there and back.

## Day 5, Thursday 22 May: Osprey Island

Kayak trip around the sea caves with Bluefin Paddle, 2 pm to 5 pm, 55 dollars per person. The trip is cancelled if the wind is stronger than 25 kilometres per hour; they refund in full.

## Day 6, Friday 23 May: home

Take the 9:30 am ferry back to Wrenhaven, then the 12:20 Coastline Express, arriving at Port Aldermoor Central at 15:35.

## Budget and notes

The total budget for two people is 1,400 dollars. Travel insurance was bought from Harrowgate Mutual, policy number HM-558-203, and covers cancellations up to 2,000 dollars. Bram is allergic to shellfish. The emergency contact is Hollis Varga on 555-0178.
