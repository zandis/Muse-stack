# Pinecrest Court residents' handbook

## 1. Welcome

This handbook is for everyone who lives at Pinecrest Court, 14 Larkspur Lane. It is a fictional document written for testing; the building, the people and the phone numbers are invented. Pinecrest Court has 36 flats on six floors. The building was finished in 1998 and the roof was renewed in 2027. The residents' committee meets every second month in the ground-floor common room, and every resident may attend.

## 2. Getting in and out

The front door opens with a fob. Each flat gets three fobs; a replacement costs 18 dollars from the building manager. The side gate on Thistle Row is locked between 11 pm and 6 am. Deliveries are left in the parcel lockers in the lobby, and the locker code is sent by text message. Parcels not collected within 5 days are returned to the sender. Visitors use the intercom: press the flat number and then the bell key.

## 3. Rubbish and recycling

General rubbish is collected on Mondays and Thursdays. Paper and card are collected on Wednesdays. Glass goes in the green bank beside the side gate and must not be put out after 8 pm because of the noise. Food waste goes in the brown caddies, which are emptied on Fridays. Large items such as mattresses are not collected by the city; book a pickup with the building manager, which costs 25 dollars per item.

## 4. Noise and neighbours

Quiet hours are from 10:30 pm to 7 am on weekdays and from 11:30 pm to 9 am at weekends. Drilling and other noisy work is allowed only between 9 am and 6 pm from Monday to Saturday, and never on Sundays. If you plan a party, tell the flats beside, above and below you at least two days before. Musical instruments may be played until 9 pm.

## 5. Laundry room

The laundry room is in the basement beside the storage lockers. It has four washing machines and three dryers. A wash costs 2 dollars and a dry costs 1.50 dollars, paid with the laundry card. The room is open from 7 am to 10 pm. Clothes left for more than 3 hours after a cycle ends may be moved to the shelf by the door. The lint filter of each dryer must be cleaned after every use.

## 6. Heating and water

The heating runs from 15 October to 15 April and is included in the building fee. Radiators are bled by the building manager every autumn; do not open the valves yourself. Hot water is heated centrally to 60 degrees. If you have no hot water for more than 2 hours, call the building manager. The main water valve for each flat is in the cupboard under the kitchen sink.

## 7. Bicycles, cars and scooters

Bicycles are kept in the racks in the courtyard, not in the stairwell or the lift. There are 12 parking spaces behind the building; each costs 40 dollars a month and there is a waiting list kept by the treasurer. Visitors may park for up to 4 hours in the two marked visitor spaces. Electric scooters must not be charged in the corridors because of the fire risk.

## 8. Fire safety

The fire alarm is tested on the first Tuesday of each month at 11 am. If the alarm sounds at any other time, leave by the stairs and gather at the assembly point, which is the bus shelter on Thistle Row. Do not use the lift. Each flat has a smoke detector whose battery the resident replaces once a year. Fire doors on the landings must never be propped open. Barbecues are not allowed on balconies.

## 9. Balconies and windows

Nothing may be hung over the balcony rail. Planters must sit inside the rail. Windows are cleaned on the outside twice a year, in April and October, by a contractor paid from the building fee. Satellite dishes need written permission from the committee.

## 10. Pets

Residents may keep one cat or one small dog, in line with their lease. Dogs are kept on a lead in the lobby, the lift and the courtyard. Owners clean up after their pets at once. Complaints about barking go first to the owner and then to the building manager.

## 11. Common room and roof terrace

The common room on the ground floor can be booked for private events for 30 dollars per evening, through the building manager, at least one week ahead. It holds 25 people. The roof terrace is open from 8 am to 10 pm from May to September. Glass bottles are not allowed on the roof terrace, and the door must be closed behind you because it sets off an alarm if left open for more than 3 minutes.

## 12. Repairs and faults

Faults inside your flat are reported to your landlord or, for flats managed by the building, to the building manager, Ines Calloway, at repairs@larkspur-lane.example. Faults in shared areas, such as a broken stair light, can be reported by anyone. The out-of-hours number for emergencies such as a flood or a stuck lift is 555-0142. The lift is serviced four times a year.

## 13. Moving in and out

Book the goods lift for a move at least 3 days ahead; moves are allowed between 8 am and 7 pm. Protect the lift walls with the blankets kept in the manager's office. A moving deposit of 150 dollars is taken and returned if nothing is damaged. Return all fobs and the laundry card when you leave.

## 14. Money

The building fee is paid monthly by standing order. It covers the lift, heating, water, cleaning of shared areas, window cleaning, rubbish collection and building insurance. Contents insurance for your own belongings is your own responsibility. The accounts are shown to residents at the annual general meeting each November.
