# Residential lease: Flat 4B, 14 Larkspur Lane

This is a fictional document written for testing. No person, address or company in it is real.

## Parties and property

The landlord is Tobias Renfield. The tenant is Mira Okonjo-Vale. The property is Flat 4B on the fourth floor of 14 Larkspur Lane, Port Aldermoor. The flat has two bedrooms and one storage locker in the basement, numbered B-17.

## Term

The lease starts on 1 September 2031 and ends on 31 August 2033. Either side must give 75 days of written notice before the end date if they do not want to renew. If nobody gives notice, the lease continues month to month on the same terms.

## Rent

The monthly rent is 1,450 dollars. Rent is due on the 3rd day of each month and is paid by bank transfer to the landlord's account at Greywater Cooperative Bank. If rent is more than 6 days late, a late fee of 45 dollars is added for that month.

The rent may be raised once, on 1 September 2032, by no more than 4 percent.

## Deposit

The tenant pays a deposit of 2,900 dollars, which equals two months of rent. The landlord returns the deposit within 21 days after the tenant moves out, less the cost of any damage beyond normal wear.

## Utilities

The tenant pays for electricity and internet. The landlord pays for water, heating and the building fee. The building fee covers the lift, the stairwell lights and rubbish collection.

## Pets and changes

One cat or one small dog is allowed with a one-time pet fee of 120 dollars. The tenant may paint the walls only in white or light grey, and must ask in writing before drilling into the tiled walls of the bathroom.

## Repairs

The tenant reports faults to the building manager, Ines Calloway, by email at repairs@larkspur-lane.example. Urgent faults, such as a burst pipe, are reported by phone on 555-0142. The landlord fixes urgent faults within 24 hours and other faults within 10 working days.

## Ending early

The tenant may end the lease early after the first 12 months by paying a break fee equal to one month of rent and giving 75 days of notice.
