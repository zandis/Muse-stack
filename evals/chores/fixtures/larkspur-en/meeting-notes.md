# Pinecrest Residents' Committee: meeting notes, 12 March 2031

Fictional notes written for testing. The people and the building do not exist.

Present: Hollis Varga (chair), Ines Calloway (building manager), Dario Penhallow (treasurer), Suvi Aaltonen, Mira Okonjo-Vale. Apologies: Bram Oyelaran.

## 1. Lift replacement

Ines reported that the lift failed four times in February. Two quotes were received: Corvid Lifts quoted 38,400 dollars and Tessel Elevators quoted 41,750 dollars. The committee voted 4 to 1 to accept the Corvid Lifts quote because it includes a five-year service plan. Work starts on 9 June 2031 and should take three weeks. During the work, residents on floors five and six may use the goods lift between 7 am and 9 pm.

## 2. Budget

Dario said the reserve fund holds 62,300 dollars. After the lift is paid for, 23,900 dollars will remain. To rebuild the reserve, the monthly building fee rises from 85 dollars to 96 dollars from 1 July 2031. Suvi voted against the increase and asked for a cheaper cleaning contract to be considered first.

## 3. Bicycle storage

Suvi proposed turning the unused boiler room into bicycle storage for 30 bicycles. The estimated cost is 3,200 dollars for racks and a new lock. The committee agreed to ask residents by survey; the survey closes on 30 April 2031.

## 4. Garden

The spring planting day is Saturday 5 April 2031 at 10 am. Hollis will order compost and 40 lavender plants. Volunteers should bring gloves.

## Actions

- Ines: sign the contract with Corvid Lifts by 21 March 2031.
- Dario: send the notice about the new building fee by 1 May 2031.
- Suvi: write the bicycle storage survey by 28 March 2031.
- Hollis: order compost and plants by 29 March 2031.

Next meeting: 14 May 2031 at 7 pm in the ground-floor common room.
