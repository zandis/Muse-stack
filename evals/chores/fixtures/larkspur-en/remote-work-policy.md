# Quillfeather Studio: remote work policy

Fictional company policy written for testing. Version 3, in force from 1 February 2031. Owner: Petra Lindqvist, operations lead.

## Who may work remotely

All staff who have passed their three-month probation may work remotely up to 3 days a week. New staff work in the studio every day during probation unless their manager agrees otherwise in writing.

## Core hours

Everyone must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:30 studio time, Monday to Thursday. Friday has no core hours. The team meeting is on Tuesdays at 10:15 and is attended in person or by video.

## Studio days

Each team picks one anchor day a week when the whole team is in the studio. The design team's anchor day is Wednesday and the engineering team's anchor day is Thursday.

## Equipment

The studio lends one laptop and one monitor for home use. Staff may claim up to 250 dollars once every two years for a chair or desk, with a receipt, through the expenses form. Home internet costs are not reimbursed.

## Working from another country

Working from another country is allowed for at most 20 working days in a calendar year and needs approval from the operations lead at least 14 days in advance, because of tax and insurance rules.

## Security

Work laptops must use the studio's VPN on any network outside the studio. Client files stay on the studio drive and must not be copied to personal devices. A lost or stolen laptop is reported to it-help@quillfeather.example within 2 hours.

## Requests and review

Requests for a different arrangement go to your manager first, then to the operations lead. This policy is reviewed every January.
