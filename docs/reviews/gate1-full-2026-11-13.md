# Gate 1, full run against real providers

Run by the owner on the VM on 2026-11-13, 17:26 to 18:43 Asia/Taipei. 133 runs, 922 requests, 743,426 tokens, US$0. The per-run data is in `docs/reviews/data/gate1-full-2026-11-13.csv` (ids, counts and codes; no prompts, answers or document text). The figures below are computed from that file.

Which router build answered is not recorded in the file. The live router on the VM was known to be an older build earlier the same day.

## Result

| Skill | Runs | Passed its own check | Right by expected facts | 95% interval for "right" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Answer over my files | 67 | 67 (100%) | **65 (97%)** | 90% to 99% |
| Research report | 66 | 54 (82%) | **27 (41%)** | 30% to 53% |

**Answer over my files passes gate 1.** **Research report fails it.**

The gate was written as "80% pass their own check". By that wording the research skill would pass at 82% while being right 41% of the time: its own check passed 30 wrong reports. The gate must be judged on "right by expected facts"; the harness is being changed to say so.

## Answer over my files

- 2.2 requests and 2,313 tokens a run; median 1.4 seconds; no empty answers.
- Traditional Chinese 28 of 28; questions in one language over files in the other 6 of 6.
- By kind: lookup 21/21, numeric 14/14, date 7/7, long file 7/7, multi-step 6/7, not in the files 4/5.
- Two misses, both passed by the skill's own check: one multi-step question got one of two facts; one "not in the files" question was answered when it should not have been.
- Slow tail: 8 of 67 runs took 20 to 87 seconds. Four had a recorded wait on a rate limit; four were answered by one provider with no wait recorded and are unexplained.

## Research report

- 11.7 requests and 8,916 tokens a run; median 33 seconds, one run in ten over 2.5 minutes.
- 172 of 774 calls (22%) came back empty because a reasoning model used its whole output on hidden reasoning.
- Empty answers track failure: runs with none were right 9 of 13 times; runs with three or more, 7 of 34.
- By kind: not in the files 5/5, date 4/5, lookup 13/26, multi-step 2/10, long file 2/9, numeric 1/6, across languages 0/5.
- Runs answered by Gemini alone were right 5 of 6 times but took a median of 185 seconds.

## What the free allowance covered

The batch used about 900 requests in 78 minutes. Towards the end Gemini and then Groq stopped answering and the last ten runs fell to OpenRouter alone. So one person's free allowances, on the services connected that day, covered roughly 900 requests: several hundred file questions, or about 60 research reports. The report did not record the allowance state; the harness is being changed to.

## What follows

1. Lead the first release with "answer over my files". It is fast, cheap and right.
2. Hold the research report back until it passes on real providers. The fixes in progress target the two measured causes: empty answers (22% of calls) and a check that passes wrong reports (30 of 54).
3. Judge every skill on "right by expected facts", never on its own check alone.
4. Explain the slow tail before a tester sees it: record time and provider per call.
5. Cap nightly real runs well below the daily allowance.
